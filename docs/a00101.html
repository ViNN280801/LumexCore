This header ports the "keep a settings file alive" behavior of PeakExpertWeb's JSON-specific BaseConfiguration (default-key restoration, corruption backup, and per-key validation) to a form that works with any ILumexSettings implementation (INI today, JSON or others tomorrow), by driving everything through the interface's load/save/get/add contract instead of a concrete parser.
