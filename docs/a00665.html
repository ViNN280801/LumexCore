Detailed Description

LUMEX_ATTRIBUTE_* macros that spell C++ attributes portably, and the structure packing macros LUMEX_PACK_BEGIN and LUMEX_PACK_END.

Each attribute macro expands to the standard attribute when the compiled standard has it, otherwise to a GCC, Clang or MSVC extension with the same effect, and to nothing where neither exists. The macros cover nodiscard (with a reason), noreturn, noinline, packed, carries_dependency, deprecated (with or without a reason), fallthrough, maybe_unused, likely and unlikely, no_unique_address (C++20), assume (C++23) and indeterminate (C++26). LUMEX_ATTRIBUTE_MAYBE_UNUSED_VAR(...) silences an unused expression by casting it to void. LUMEX_ATTRIBUTE_PACKED is empty for MSVC, which packs through LUMEX_PACK_BEGIN(n) and LUMEX_PACK_END instead; those two work with MSVC, GCC and Clang and are empty elsewhere.
