Detailed Description

Size limits and alignment of the XPath engine.

kxpath_memory_page_size (4096) is the data size of an XPathMemoryBlock, kxpath_ast_depth_limit (1024) the deepest recursion XPathParser accepts before it reports an error, and kxpath_memory_block_alignment the alignment of every XPath allocation, the larger of the alignments needed for a double and a pointer. The constants are in lumex::xml::xpath::constants::Constants.
