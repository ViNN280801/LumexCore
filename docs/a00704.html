Detailed Description

General preprocessor helpers: LUMEX_FUNCTION_NAME, LUMEX_STRINGIZE and LUMEX_CONCAT.

LUMEX_FUNCTION_NAME is the decorated name of the enclosing function, chosen by compiler, not by target: __FUNCSIG__ with MSVC and clang-cl (_MSC_VER), __PRETTY_FUNCTION__ with GCC and Clang (MinGW included), the standard __func__ otherwise. LUMEX_STRINGIZE(x) and LUMEX_CONCAT(x, y) expand their arguments before turning them into a string literal or pasting them into one token.
