LUMEX_CONST_NUM std::int64_t lumex::applied::resource_monitor::monitor::Constants::KSTARTUP_GRACE_PERIOD_MS = 2000

Grace period the sampler waits before taking its first sample.

Gives the host application time to finish initializing before any sampling starts, and keeps a thread/instrumentation sanitizer from reporting noise for activity that happens during that initialization window.
